## Limit Order

it's an order. 
an order with a price limit.
it triggers when buy is >= limit, or sell is >= limit?

a market order has no price limit. executes immediately at whatever is available.

## resting order:
unmatched orders

## crossing the spread:
- spread is the gab between the best bid and best ask
- crossing just means an order crosses the gap
- aka, the LOWEST SELL matches the HIGHEST BUY

## crossed
- best bid is higher than the best ask
